# Chapter 21 · The Copper Arch — Image prompts
📍 The Copper Arch (Bazaar Gate) · Words 201–210

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #201 BUSTLING (4:3)
A huge copper arch engraved with camels at the entrance to a bustling medieval Baghdad bazaar; donkeys, camels, merchants and jugglers everywhere; colors half-drained to grey; three kids, an old sailor and a baby roc entering.

### Frame 2 · #202 THRONG (4:3)
A dense crowd surging through a bazaar gate, sweeping kids apart; a small hand sneaking into a boy's satchel.

### Frame 3 · #203 DIVERSE (4:3)
A boy chasing a thief through a bazaar crowd of traders from many lands: Persian, Chinese, African, Greek; rich variety of costumes.

### Frame 4 · #204 ECLECTIC (4:3)
A cluttered stall full of a stuffed crocodile, a Viking helmet, drums and kites; a thief ducking inside; a boy skidding after.

### Frame 5 · #205 VIBRANT (4:3)
A patch of bazaar bursting with color among grey stalls; a 16-year-old girl in a crimson bandana with a bandolier of throwing knives grinning while holding a stolen spark.

### Frame 6 · #206 COSMOPOLITAN (4:3)
A confident girl in a crimson bandana chatting easily with traders of many cultures while spinning a glowing spark on her finger.

### Frame 7 · #207 COMMERCE (4:3)
A girl scholar in an indigo headscarf talking seriously with a girl in a crimson bandana across a merchant's table; coins and goods around.

### Frame 8 · #208 MERCANTILE (4:3)
A secret hideout in a maze of trading warehouses; forty orphan kids each wearing a single grey feather; a girl in a crimson bandana leading the newcomers in.

### Frame 9 · #209 UBIQUITOUS (4:3)
A rooftop view of a bazaar where shadowy spies lurk on every corner, rooftop and stall; a girl in a crimson bandana pointing them out to the kids.

### Frame 0 · #210 TUMULT (wide 4:1)
A bazaar square erupting in noisy commotion; people running and shouting; spice-sellers fainting in the distance under drifting purple smoke.
