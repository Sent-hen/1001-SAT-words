# Chapter 33 · Roses with Thorny Tongues — Image prompts
📍 The Garden of Talking Roses · Words 321–330

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #321 ACERBIC (4:3)
A walled rose garden where roses have tiny faces with sharp expressions, mocking three kids entering; biting speech bubbles.

### Frame 2 · #322 CAUSTIC (4:3)
A rose with a sneering face insulting a boy who flinches; acid-green speech bubble.

### Frame 3 · #323 SARDONIC (4:3)
A rose with a scornful, sarcastic smile mocking an old sailor, a baby roc and a boy; the roses around it snickering.

### Frame 4 · #324 SCATHING (4:3)
A huge queen rose with a crown of thorns delivering a harsh speech; the words forming whip-like shapes; kids wincing.

### Frame 5 · #325 DERISIVE (4:3)
A whole garden of roses bursting into mocking laughter, petals shaking; the kids surrounded.

### Frame 6 · #326 INSOLENT (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband yelling rudely back at the roses; roses gasping in shock; a girl scholar pulling him back.

### Frame 7 · #327 SYCOPHANT (4:3)
A small rose bowing and fanning a big queen rose with a leaf, flattering it; comic exaggeration.

### Frame 8 · #328 VITRIOLIC (4:3)
Black thorny vines shooting up around the kids as a queen rose spits hateful words; menacing dark tones.

### Frame 9 · #329 PETULANT (4:3)
A girl scholar giving a sincere compliment; the queen rose turning its head away, sulking with pouty petals.

### Frame 0 · #330 DISPARAGE (wide 4:1)
Softened roses dropping a glowing spark; kids leaving the garden; beyond the wall, a roar from a zoo with shadowy animal shapes.
