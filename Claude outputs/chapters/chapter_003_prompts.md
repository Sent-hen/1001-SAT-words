# Chapter 3 · Knots of the Hushlings — Image prompts
📍 The Rope-Makers' Wharf · Words 21–30

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #21 ENSNARE (4:3)
Nighttime rope-makers' wharf; ropes soaked with black ink come alive like snakes and wrap around a boy with an orange headband, hoisting him upside-down; a girl scholar and a small boy reach for him in alarm.

### Frame 2 · #22 TENACIOUS (4:3)
A determined girl in an indigo headscarf dragged across wooden planks while gripping a boy's wrist, her teeth gritted; living ropes pulling the other way; sparks of gold flying.

### Frame 3 · #23 FUTILE (4:3)
A small boy furiously sawing at a black ink-soaked rope with a fish knife while the cut heals itself instantly; an ink djinn shouting a warning.

### Frame 4 · #24 INGENIOUS (4:3)
A girl scholar and a boy cranking a huge wooden rope-twisting wheel backward; living ropes unravelling into limp strings; an ink djinn clapping with delight.

### Frame 5 · #25 PREDICAMENT (4:3)
Three kids and a small ink djinn balanced on a single narrow plank over a dark river at night; shadowy ink creatures closing in from both ends; tense cinematic angle.

### Frame 6 · #26 ADEPT (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband doing a skillful acrobatic flip over a shadow creature on a narrow plank above the river, landing perfectly; lanterns and masts behind.

### Frame 7 · #27 EVADE (4:3)
Kids dodging and ducking under grabbing ink-shadow hands on a wharf; an ink djinn flattened like a puddle slipping between them; dynamic motion lines.

### Frame 8 · #28 RELENTLESS (4:3)
An endless wave of mouthless shadow creatures pouring over coils of rope and up wooden stairs; a frightened girl scholar looking back as she runs.

### Frame 9 · #29 DEFT (4:3)
A boy quickly tying a slipknot and cutting a rope bridge free; three kids and an ink djinn swinging across a gap over a dark river on the rope, moonlight behind.

### Frame 0 · #30 JEOPARDY (wide 4:1)
Three kids and a small ink djinn landing on a wharf before a stern stone customs house; soldiers with giant brass stamps confiscating glowing golden sparks; a tense warning glance.
