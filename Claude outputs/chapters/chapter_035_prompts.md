# Chapter 35 · Duel of Poets — Image prompts
📍 The Poets' Café · Words 341–350

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #341 RHETORIC (4:3)
A lantern-lit poets' café with a small stage; a smug poet in fine clothes performing dramatically; a crowd of listeners on cushions; three kids entering.

### Frame 2 · #342 ARTICULATE (4:3)
A girl scholar performing clearly and confidently on a poetry stage; the smug poet then pointing mockingly at the boy with an orange headband.

### Frame 3 · #343 LYRICAL (4:3)
A nervous boy on a stage finding his voice, speaking from the heart with visions of rooftops and wind swirling around him; the café going silent.

### Frame 4 · #344 CADENCE (4:3)
A crowd in a café tapping their feet and hands in rhythm as a boy performs; rhythm lines.

### Frame 5 · #345 METAPHOR (4:3)
A boy performing with arms spread; above him a vision of a great city shaped like a bird with broken wings; a girl scholar staring amazed.

### Frame 6 · #346 ALLEGORY (4:3)
A boy telling a story as images of a mute king stealing songs appear in the lamp smoke; listeners nodding knowingly.

### Frame 7 · #347 SATIRE (4:3)
A smug poet performing a mocking parody of the boy with exaggerated gestures; the crowd laughing.

### Frame 8 · #348 REBUTTAL (4:3)
A boy delivering a sharp comeback with a grin; the smug poet stunned; the crowd roaring with laughter.

### Frame 9 · #349 BANTER (4:3)
Two rivals, the boy and the poet, laughing and playfully teasing each other; the crowd cheering.

### Frame 0 · #350 ORATORY (wide 4:1)
Judges handing a glowing spark to the boy; an old waiter leaning in to whisper; a faint ghostly image of a stammering boy on the same stage long ago.
