# Chapter 91 · The Golden Gate — Image prompts
📍 The Golden Gate · Words 901–910

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #901 RESPLENDENT (4:3)
A gigantic blazing gold palace gate at dawn, too bright to look at; an army of ordinary citizens, nomads, orphans, soldiers, four brass guardians and a giant roc gathered before it.

### Frame 2 · #902 GILDED (4:3)
A close-up of the gate's gold flaking off to reveal rotten wood underneath; a girl scholar noticing.

### Frame 3 · #903 OSTENTATIOUS (4:3)
A sinister auctioneer on the gate in showy robes: twelve rings, a peacock hat and a golden cane.

### Frame 4 · #904 LAVISH (4:3)
A sinister auctioneer holding out a vision of golden palaces to the crowd as a bribe.

### Frame 5 · #905 EXTRAVAGANT (4:3)
A crowd laughing at a sinister auctioneer's over-the-top promises of gold rivers and diamond roads.

### Frame 6 · #906 GAUDY (4:3)
An old sailor in a garish neon turban mocking the auctioneer's gaudy robes; the crowd laughing.

### Frame 7 · #907 SUMPTUOUS (4:3)
A glimpse through the gate of a splendid, lavish feast table with a thousand dishes, untouched.

### Frame 8 · #908 PRETENTIOUS (4:3)
A sinister auctioneer making a fancy speech; the crowd shouting corrections; he looks flustered.

### Frame 9 · #909 SPLENDOR (4:3)
A giant red-gold roc swooping over a gate with four brass guardians charging below; the brass lion roaring; magnificent light.

### Frame 0 · #910 POMPOUS (wide 4:1)
A sinister auctioneer tumbling off a gate into a fountain; the golden gates cracking open; the army pouring through.
