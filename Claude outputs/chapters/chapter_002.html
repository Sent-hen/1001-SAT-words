CHAPTER 2A DJINN IN THE FISH BASKET

📍 The Fish Market
District 1: Harbor of a Thousand Sails
Words 11–20
🎨 Image style prefix & character key (added automatically by “Copy full prompt”)

Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text. Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.

FRAME 1 · WORD #11📍 STATION 1 · ARCH
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

A busy nighttime fish market in mythic Baghdad under striped awnings; a teenage boy with an orange headband and a girl in an indigo headscarf crash into an ice-basket of silver fish; a small black ink djinn pops out; a stout old fishmonger woman stares, jaw dropped in disbelief.

INCREDULOUSadj.unable to believe something🔊 Sound-hook: in-CRED-ulous ≈ “gives it no CREDit”: you can't believe it
STORY

SPLOOSH! Zayn and Layla crash into a basket of fish. The fishmonger, Umm Samak, drops her scale-knife, incredulous: two kids and a talking blob of ink just fell out of the sky. She simply can't believe her eyes.

LINK

LOCI

Station 1 · ARCH. Under the swordfish-skull arch, a giant fish hangs with its jaw on the floor, refusing to believe what it sees = INCREDULOUS.

FRAME 2 · WORD #12📍 STATION 2 · STEP
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

A playful ink djinn transforming mid-air through silly shapes (flying fish, teapot, crowned camel) above fish stalls; a delighted 10-year-old boy with a missing front tooth and an oversized lime-green cap laughs and points.

WHIMSICALadj.playfully odd and unpredictable🔊 Sound-hook: WHIM-sical ≈ “WHIM-SICLE”: a popsicle that changes on a whim
STORY

Qalam shows off. He becomes a flying fish, then a teapot, then a camel wearing a crown, just for fun. A little boy in a huge green cap giggles. It's Tariq, Zayn's kid brother. “Your djinn is so whimsical!” Whimsical means playfully odd and unpredictable.

LINK

12→13 · The whim-sicle melts into a puddle that rises up as a tiny ELEPHANT in a tuxedo. He bows to a queen and delivers a speech so beautiful the fish cry: “HELLO, QUEEN-t!” (Action + Substitution: elo-QUENT ≈ hello-queen).

LOCI

Station 2 · STEP. On the slippery scaled step, a popsicle keeps changing color and flavor on a whim; step on it and you slide somewhere random = WHIMSICAL.

FRAME 3 · WORD #13📍 STATION 3 · LAMP
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

A girl scholar in an indigo headscarf standing on a fish crate, one hand raised, speaking with confidence to a frightened crowd of fishermen; shadowy ink creatures creeping in at the edges; lanterns being covered.

ELOQUENTadj.speaking beautifully and persuasively🔊 Sound-hook: ELO-quent ≈ “HELLO, QUEEN”: a speech fit to greet a queen
STORY

Hushlings seep into the market and people start to scream. Layla climbs onto a crate and gives a calm, powerful speech: “Stay together. Hide the lanterns. They hunt light!” The panicking crowd listens. She's eloquent: she speaks beautifully and persuasively.

LINK

13→14 · The tuxedo elephant's beautiful speech (eloquent) makes his crown split open like a lid. Inside is a dark stone CRYPT full of whispering riddles written in secret code (Substitution: CRYPT-ic).

LOCI

Station 3 · LAMP. The pufferfish lantern on the left puffs up and delivers a stirring, beautiful speech to the whole market = ELOQUENT.

FRAME 4 · WORD #14📍 STATION 4 · RUG
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

Close-up of a small ink djinn with glowing golden eyes speaking in mysterious swirling calligraphy letters that float in the air; a confused teenage boy scratching his head; ice and fish around them.

CRYPTICadj.mysterious; hard to understand🔊 Sound-hook: CRYPT-ic ≈ like a CRYPT: dark, sealed and mysterious
STORY

“Where did the other sparks fall?” Zayn asks. Qalam's eyes glow: “Ten with fins, ten with feathers, ten where the silent bell sleeps.” Zayn groans. “Can you talk normal?” That clue is cryptic: mysterious and hard to understand.

LINK

14→15 · Out of the riddle-crypt (cryptic) climbs a huge fluffy BULL in a sequin jacket, “A-FAB-BULL”, who hugs everyone and asks how their day was (Out of proportion + Substitution: aff-ABLE ≈ a-fab-bull).

LOCI

Station 4 · RUG. In the crushed-ice floor, a stone crypt lid is frozen, carved with a riddle nobody can read = CRYPTIC.

FRAME 5 · WORD #15📍 STATION 5 · HEART
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

A cheerful 10-year-old boy in a lime-green cap chatting happily with smiling fish-sellers who point in one direction; warm lantern light; baskets of silver fish.

AFFABLEadj.friendly and easy to talk to🔊 Sound-hook: aff-ABLE ≈ “A FAB BULL”: a fabulous, friendly bull anyone can talk to
STORY

Tariq trots from stall to stall, chatting and grinning. Within a minute, every fishmonger knows his name and three of them point the way to the rope wharf. “My brother's affable,” Zayn says. “Friendly and easy to talk to. Annoyingly.”

LINK

15→16 · The fab bull hugs a nervous kid so hard he shoots onto the ice wearing brand-new skates. He's “NEW ON ICE” and wobbling wildly, arms flailing (Action + Substitution: NOV-ice ≈ new-on-ice).

LOCI

Station 5 · HEART. Sitting in the giant ice-basket at the center is a fluffy bull in a sequin jacket, waving at everyone and happily chatting = AFFABLE.

FRAME 6 · WORD #16📍 STATION 6 · PILLAR
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

A teenage boy clumsily holding a glowing golden spark while a small ink djinn collapses into a lumpy blob; the boy looks embarrassed; a girl scholar facepalms in the background.

NOVICEn.a beginner🔊 Sound-hook: NOV-ice ≈ “NEW on ICE”: a beginner wobbling on skates
STORY

Zayn grabs a new spark and shouts “AFFABLE!”, but he says it with an angry face. Qalam turns into a lumpy blob. “You're a novice,” Qalam sighs. “A beginner. Mean the word, don't just shout it.” Zayn tries again, smiling. The spark clicks into the Satchel.

LINK

16→17 · The wobbling new skater (novice) crashes into a mountain of FRILLY party hats, confetti and silly frills (Out of proportion). Nothing serious survives inside the frill-pile (Substitution: FRIV-olous ≈ frilly).

LOCI

Station 6 · PILLAR. Clinging to the octopus-hung pillar is a beginner skater, legs sliding apart, too new to stand = NOVICE.

FRAME 7 · WORD #17📍 STATION 7 · WINDOW
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

A small ink djinn shaped like a ridiculous giant party hat on a little boy's head; a girl scholar glaring and scolding a grinning boy with an orange headband; dark shadows approaching behind stalls.

FRIVOLOUSadj.silly; not serious🔊 Sound-hook: FRIV-olous ≈ “FRILLY”: all frills, nothing serious
STORY

With Hushlings two stalls away, Zayn makes Qalam become a giant party hat for Tariq. “Seriously?!” Layla hisses. “That was frivolous: silly, not serious. Save your tricks for things that matter!”

LINK

17→18 · A furious MOLE bursts up through the frilly party hats (frivolous), DIGGING IN with fists raised, angry at the unfair mess on his lawn (Action + Substitution: in-DIG-nant ≈ in-dig).

LOCI

Station 7 · WINDOW. The fishmonger's back window is stuffed with frilly party hats and confetti, silly junk blocking the view = FRIVOLOUS.

FRAME 8 · WORD #18📍 STATION 8 · STAIR
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

A shadowy mouthless ink creature touching an old fishmonger woman who turns grey like stone; a small boy in a green cap stamps his foot with an angry, tearful face; color draining from the scene.

INDIGNANTadj.angry at something unfair🔊 Sound-hook: in-DIG-nant ≈ “IN-DIG”: a mole digging in, furious at injustice
STORY

A Hushling brushes Umm Samak and she turns grey and silent. Tariq stamps his foot, indignant: “That's not fair! She didn't do anything!” Indignant means angry at something unfair.

LINK

18→19 · The furious mole (indignant) pops up in the middle of a noisy party of seagulls led by GREG, a friendly gull who's never without his giant crowd of friends (Substitution: GREG-arious ≈ Greg-and-crew).

LOCI

Station 8 · STAIR. Halfway up the rope-ladder stair, an angry mole shakes his tiny fist at the unfair Hushlings = INDIGNANT.

FRAME 9 · WORD #19📍 STATION 9 · BALCONY
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

A joyful swarm of seagulls, cats and laughing fishermen surging around confused shadow creatures in a lantern-lit fish market; a small boy in a green cap whistling with two fingers.

GREGARIOUSadj.loving company; very social🔊 Sound-hook: GREG-arious ≈ “GREG and his crew”: never alone
STORY

Tariq whistles. Seagulls, cats and a hundred fishermen swarm in, laughing and shouting, a joyful mob that confuses the Hushlings. “Everyone here loves a crowd,” Tariq grins. Gregarious means loving company; very social.

LINK

19→20 · Greg's giant crowd (gregarious) piles into the PRAG-MATIC 3000, a practical machine that simply fixes each problem with the tool that works: a plank for a gap, a rope for a wall (Action + Substitution: PRAG-matic ≈ practical auto-matic).

LOCI

Station 9 · BALCONY. The net-loft balcony is packed with Greg the seagull and a hundred friends, all chatting and waving = GREGARIOUS.

FRAME 0 · WORD #20📍 STATION 10 · TOP
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · WIDE 4:1

Rooftop at night above a fish market; a practical girl scholar pointing the way along the roofs; a boy, his little brother and an ink djinn following; below, coils of rope on a wharf beginning to twitch and slither like snakes.

PRAGMATICadj.practical; focused on what works🔊 Sound-hook: PRAG-matic ≈ PRACTICAL + auto-MATIC: whatever works
STORY

Layla takes charge: “Arguing won't help. We need rope and a way out.” Practical as always, she leads them up to the roof. Pragmatic means focused on what works. But below them the rope wharf's ropes start to twitch… and slither.

LINK

20→21 · The Prag-matic 3000 (pragmatic) fires a practical net, but the net comes alive and ENSNARES a whole blue whale, wrapping it up like a gift (Out of proportion + Action). IN a SNARE = ensnare.

LOCI

Station 10 · TOP. The fish weathervane on the roof has been fitted with a practical toolbelt and ladder, turned into something useful = PRAGMATIC.
