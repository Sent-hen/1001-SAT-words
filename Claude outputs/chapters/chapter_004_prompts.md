# Chapter 4 · The Tax on Truth — Image prompts
📍 The Customs House · Words 31–40

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #31 LEVY (4:3)
A fat, greedy customs chief in an official turban blocking a stone doorway, holding up a notice reading nothing legible; three kids and a small ink djinn looking outraged.

### Frame 2 · #32 ARBITRARY (4:3)
A smug customs chief rolling a pair of dice on a desk to decide a tax, while clerks shrug; a boy with an orange headband pointing at him in outrage.

### Frame 3 · #33 BUREAUCRACY (4:3)
A small boy in a lime-green cap buried under towering piles of paper forms and stamps in an old customs hall; bored clerks stamping endlessly; comic exaggeration.

### Frame 4 · #34 SCRUTINIZE (4:3)
A customs chief peering through an oversized magnifying lens at a glowing golden spark, one eye huge; three impatient kids behind the desk.

### Frame 5 · #35 CONTRABAND (4:3)
A small boy in a green cap pulling open a sack behind a customs desk to reveal smuggled silk, saffron and a stolen glowing spark; the corrupt chief sweating.

### Frame 6 · #36 EXTORT (4:3)
A greedy customs chief leaning in close and whispering a threat to a scared girl scholar, holding out his palm; shadows of a masked vizier on the wall behind him.

### Frame 7 · #37 DUBIOUS (4:3)
A sweating customs chief with shifty eyes giving a nervous excuse; three kids crossing their arms skeptically; a barred vault window behind them.

### Frame 8 · #38 INTEGRITY (4:3)
A brave young female clerk slamming her stamp down on a desk and refusing a bribe; the corrupt chief shocked; the kids cheering behind her.

### Frame 9 · #39 VENAL (4:3)
A corrupt customs chief dangling glowing sparks in front of greedy guards on a balcony; the guards' eyes turning into coins; kids backing away.

### Frame 0 · #40 METICULOUS (wide 4:1)
A girl scholar with round brass spectacles tracing a finger down a huge ledger, finding the incriminating line; guards seizing the corrupt chief; through a window, a dark shipyard of skeletal hulls.
