# Chapter 25 · The Book That Bit Back — Image prompts
📍 The Booksellers' Souq · Words 241–250

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #241 ERUDITE (4:3)
A narrow bookseller street with books stacked to the ceiling and rolling ladders; a very old scholar with thick spectacles peering over a mountain of books; a girl scholar in awe.

### Frame 2 · #242 PEDANTIC (4:3)
A fussy old bookseller wagging a finger and correcting details; a boy with an orange headband rolling his eyes.

### Frame 3 · #243 ESOTERIC (4:3)
An old scholar unrolling a scroll covered in mysterious glowing symbols; a girl scholar leaning close, recognizing them.

### Frame 4 · #244 TOME (4:3)
An enormous leather book as big as a door opening its pages like jaws and snapping at a startled girl scholar; the old bookseller jumping back.

### Frame 5 · #245 ANNOTATE (4:3)
A girl scholar gently reading to a calmed book; close-up of elegant handwritten notes in a margin, glowing faintly.

### Frame 6 · #246 ARCHIVE (4:3)
A hidden underground archive of thousands of rolled scrolls in honeycomb shelves; an old bookseller leading the kids with a lantern.

### Frame 7 · #247 SCHOLARLY (4:3)
A girl scholar surrounded by open scrolls, cross-referencing with focus by lamplight; finding a crossed-out name.

### Frame 8 · #248 PROLIFIC (4:3)
An old bookseller gesturing proudly at a wall of three hundred books he wrote himself, one gap on the shelf where a book was stolen.

### Frame 9 · #249 VERBOSE (4:3)
An old bookseller talking on and on with speech bubbles filling the whole panel; a boy asleep standing up.

### Frame 0 · #250 LITERATE (wide 4:1)
A boy looking embarrassed at a page of text he can't read; a girl scholar gently offering to teach him; an auction poster with a drawing of a satchel.
