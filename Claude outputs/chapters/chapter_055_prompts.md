# Chapter 55 · The Clockwork Army — Image prompts
📍 The Clocktower of the Automata · Words 541–550

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #541 AUTOMATON (4:3)
A tall clocktower interior full of gears and brass automaton soldiers, horses and birds marching; kids hiding behind a gear.

### Frame 2 · #542 SYSTEMATIC (4:3)
Brass soldiers marching row after row in perfectly organized formation through a gear-filled hall.

### Frame 3 · #543 SYNCHRONIZE (4:3)
Brass soldiers stepping in perfect sync with a huge swinging pendulum: tick left, tock right.

### Frame 4 · #544 METHODICAL (4:3)
A girl scholar counting ticks methodically on her fingers, spotting a gap in the rhythm.

### Frame 5 · #545 UNIFORM (4:3)
A thousand identical brass soldiers with the same mouthless mask faces, all the same.

### Frame 6 · #546 CONFORMITY (4:3)
A chilling image of the city's people all dressed identically, marching in step; a girl scholar whispering in fear.

### Frame 7 · #547 AUTONOMOUS (4:3)
One small brass automaton stopping, turning its head curiously, and pointing the kids to a secret path.

### Frame 8 · #548 SEQUENCE (4:3)
Kids leaping from gear to gear in a precise sequence through the clock's giant spinning mechanism.

### Frame 9 · #549 INTRICATE (4:3)
The heart of a vast intricate clock mechanism; an inventor and an old sailor chained inside, calling to the kids.

### Frame 0 · #550 REDUNDANT (wide 4:1)
A boy pulling out a single gear; a whole brass army freezing mid-step; chains falling off an old sailor and an inventor; the tower shuddering.
