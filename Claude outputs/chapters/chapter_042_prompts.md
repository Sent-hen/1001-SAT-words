# Chapter 42 · Moat of Monsters — Image prompts
📍 The Moat Bridge · Words 411–420

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #411 LOOM (4:3)
Giant eels, ink-eyed crocodiles and a house-sized squid looming out of a castle moat around a drawbridge; kids small and exposed.

### Frame 2 · #412 LURK (4:3)
Eyes glinting under the gaps of wooden drawbridge planks; a boy stepping carefully, sweating.

### Frame 3 · #413 DAUNTING (4:3)
A long drawbridge stretching over a moat of monsters; kids looking at it with gulps; a boy thinking wistfully of his brother.

### Frame 4 · #414 VALOR (4:3)
A girl in a crimson bandana drawing throwing knives and leading forty orphan kids in a brave charge across a drawbridge.

### Frame 5 · #415 COWER (4:3)
A small ink djinn transformed into a fearsome black ink dragon with golden eyes; crocodiles crouching and cowering.

### Frame 6 · #416 INTIMIDATE (4:3)
A house-sized squid rising higher, tentacles spread to blot out the sun, glaring at the kids.

### Frame 7 · #417 HIDEOUS (4:3)
Close-up of a hideous seven-eyed warty squid face; a baby roc hiding behind an old sailor's legs.

### Frame 8 · #418 GALLANT (4:3)
An old sailor bowing gallantly to a giant squid and beginning a story; the squid tilting its head to listen.

### Frame 9 · #419 REPEL (4:3)
Orphan kids with torches driving back giant eels; a girl scholar shouting as a spark flies into her glowing satchel.

### Frame 0 · #420 PLUNGE (wide 4:1)
A giant squid happily plunging back into a moat; a drawbridge dropping; beyond it, barracks full of grey stone soldiers standing motionless.
