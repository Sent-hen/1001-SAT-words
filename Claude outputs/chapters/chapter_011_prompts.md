# Chapter 11 · The Bridge That Floats — Image prompts
📍 The Bridge of Boats (East End) · Words 101–110

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #101 TENUOUS (4:3)
A long bridge made of wooden boats tied side by side across a wide river at dawn, ropes fraying; three kids, an old sailor pushing a wheelbarrow with a giant egg, and an ink djinn about to step on.

### Frame 2 · #102 TRAVERSE (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband hopping confidently from boat to boat across the river; an old sailor behind carefully pushing a wheelbarrow with a huge speckled egg.

### Frame 3 · #103 VACILLATE (4:3)
A girl scholar in an indigo headscarf frozen at a gap between two boats, one foot forward then back, anxious; water rushing below.

### Frame 4 · #104 EQUILIBRIUM (4:3)
A girl scholar holding a long balance-pole formed from an ink djinn, arms wide, walking steadily across a narrow plank between boats.

### Frame 5 · #105 APPREHENSIVE (4:3)
Kids on a bridge of boats staring anxiously upstream where dark boats full of shadow creatures drift toward them through river mist.

### Frame 6 · #106 STEADFAST (4:3)
An old sailor bracing his feet at a bridge knot, gripping two ropes as boats buck in the current; kids running past him.

### Frame 7 · #107 TREPIDATION (4:3)
A small boy in a green cap creeping forward nervously along a lurching bridge of boats, eyes wide, as shadow boats ram the bridge.

### Frame 8 · #108 NAVIGATE (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband leading the way, pointing a path across tilted boats and ropes; others following in a line.

### Frame 9 · #109 FLUCTUATE (4:3)
A bridge of boats rising and falling on big waves; kids crouching, holding ropes; river spray everywhere.

### Frame 0 · #110 COLLABORATE (wide 4:1)
Three kids and an old sailor working together to pull a rope and tie knots, reaching large barges with waterwheels; a giant egg in a wheelbarrow cracking with golden light.
