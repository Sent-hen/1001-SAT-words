📍 The Shipyard

FRAME 2 · WORD #42📍 STATION 2 · STEP
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

Wind whistling through skeletal ship hulls at night, a lantern swinging by itself, a small frightened boy in a green cap clutching his older brother; blue-grey spooky lighting.

EERIEadj.strange and frightening🔊 Sound-hook: EER-ie ≈ “EAR-Y”: a giant ghost-ear whispering spooky sounds
STORY

Wind howls through the ribs of the hulls like voices. Ooooo. A lantern swings on its own. “This place is eerie,” Tariq whispers, gripping Zayn's satchel. Eerie means strange and frightening.

LINK

42→43 · The ghost-ear (eerie) is plugged with a RUDIMENTARY ROOT: a basic, unfinished carrot-root stick figure, only one stroke drawn (Substitution: RUDI-mentary ≈ root-y; Exaggeration of simplicity).

LOCI

Station 2 · STEP. On the rotten gangplank step, a giant floating ear whispers “ooooo” as you step on it = EERIE.

FRAME 3 · WORD #43📍 STATION 3 · LAMP
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

A boy proudly holding up a crude weapon made of a stick with a nail; a girl scholar sighing and rolling her eyes; shipyard sawdust and tools around.

RUDIMENTARYadj.basic; undeveloped🔊 Sound-hook: RUDI-mentary ≈ “ROOT-y”: just the root, not the whole plant
STORY

Zayn builds a weapon: a stick with a nail. Layla sighs. “That's rudimentary.” Rudimentary means basic, undeveloped. It's the simplest possible version.

LINK

43→44 · The simple stick-root (rudimentary) is stacked on top of a PRECARIOUS PRE-CAR tower, a tower of pre-cars (wheels, seats, horns) wobbling a mile high and ready to fall (Out of proportion + Substitution: pre-CAR-ious).

LOCI

Station 3 · LAMP. The storm lantern on the left has been replaced by a candle stuck in a potato. As basic as it gets = RUDIMENTARY.

FRAME 4 · WORD #44📍 STATION 4 · RUG

A small boy in a green cap standing on top of a wobbly, swaying stack of barrels in a moonlit shipyard; his brother below reaching up in panic.

PRECARIOUSadj.dangerously unstable🔊 Sound-hook: pre-CAR-ious ≈ a tower of CARS teetering in the sky
STORY

Tariq climbs a stack of barrels for a view. The pile sways, creaking, one barrel sliding… “Get down, that's precarious!” Precarious means dangerously unstable.

LINK

44→45 · The teetering car-tower (precarious) falls onto a DERELICT DARE-RELIC, an abandoned relic nobody dares touch, left to rust in the weeds (Substitution: DER-elict ≈ dare-relic).

LOCI

Station 4 · RUG. On the sawdust floor, a mile-high tower of barrels sways, ready to fall = PRECARIOUS.

FRAME 5 · WORD #45📍 STATION 5 · HEART
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

A huge abandoned dhow ship with tattered sails and cobwebs, its brass name plate reading nothing legible; three kids staring up; faint snoring bubbles drifting from a porthole.

DERELICTadj.abandoned and run-down🔊 Sound-hook: DER-elict ≈ “DARE-RELIC”: a relic nobody dares touch, left to rot
STORY

At the center stands the biggest ship of all, abandoned for years, its sails in rags. Its name plate reads THE WANDERING PEARL. It's derelict: abandoned and run-down. And something inside it is snoring.

LINK

45→46 · The abandoned relic (derelict) is dragged by a CUMBERSOME CUCUMBER-SUMO, a giant cucumber sumo wrestler carrying a piano, an anchor and a bathtub. Too bulky to handle (Substitution: CUM-ber-some ≈ cucumber-sumo).

LOCI

Station 5 · HEART. The giant hull at the center is abandoned, full of cobwebs and rags. A relic nobody dares enter = DERELICT.

FRAME 6 · WORD #46📍 STATION 6 · PILLAR
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

Three kids straining to drag a huge awkward iron anchor across a shipyard floor, tangled in ropes; comic effort sweat drops.

CUMBERSOMEadj.bulky and hard to handle🔊 Sound-hook: CUM-ber-some ≈ “CUCUMBER-SUMO”: a sumo carrying a giant cucumber
STORY

They try to drag a huge anchor to barricade the door against Hushlings. It's heavy, awkward and snags on everything. “This is so cumbersome!” Cumbersome means bulky and hard to handle.

LINK

46→47 · The bulky cucumber (cumbersome) is used as a brick to FORTIFY a FORT: the fort gets thicker walls, forty towers and iron bars (Substitution: FORT-ify ≈ make it a fort).

LOCI

Station 6 · PILLAR. A sumo wrestler hugs the scaffold pillar, trying to carry a giant awkward cucumber around it = CUMBERSOME.

FRAME 7 · WORD #47📍 STATION 7 · WINDOW
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

Kids hammering planks over holes in an old ship's hull while shadow creatures press in; an ink djinn stretched across a porthole like a shield.

FORTIFYv.to strengthen🔊 Sound-hook: FORT-ify ≈ “make it a FORT”
STORY

Hushlings seep in through the cracks. The kids fortify the ship: planks nailed over holes, barrels stacked at the door, Qalam stretched across the porthole. To fortify is to strengthen.

LINK

47→48 · The walls of the fort (fortify) are patched with MAKESHIFT MAKE-SHIFTS: socks, pancakes and chewing gum, thrown together at the last minute (Exaggeration + Substitution: make-SHIFT ≈ make-do in a shift).

LOCI

Station 7 · WINDOW. The porthole is bricked over into a tiny fort with towers and a flag = FORTIFY.

FRAME 8 · WORD #48📍 STATION 8 · STAIR
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · 4:3

MAKESHIFTadj.temporary, thrown together🔊 Sound-hook: MAKE-SHIFT ≈ make it in a SHIFT: quick, temporary, whatever's handy
STORY

With no real weapons, they make do: a bucket helmet, a pan shield, a broom spear. Everything is makeshift: temporary, thrown together from whatever's lying around.

LINK

48→49 · The broom-and-bucket army (makeshift) runs into a FORMIDABLE FORMULA-BULL, a bull made of math formulas, huge, powerful and terrifying to face (Substitution: FORM-idable ≈ formula-bull; Out of proportion).

LOCI

Station 8 · STAIR. The scaffold ladder-stair is made of spoons, rope and pancakes tied together = MAKESHIFT.

FRAME 9 · WORD #49📍 STATION 9 · BALCONY

STORY

LINK

49→50 · The formula-bull (formidable) is charged by an INTREPID IN-TRIP-ID kid who trips on purpose into the bull's path, fearless and adventurous, like a knight on a trip (Substitution: in-TREP-id ≈ in-trip).

LOCI

Station 9 · BALCONY. Leaning over the scaffolding balcony, a huge bull made of glowing formulas snorts steam = FORMIDABLE.

FRAME 0 · WORD #50📍 STATION 10 · TOP
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT · WIDE 4:1

A boy in a bucket helmet fearlessly charging a giant shadow creature and ringing a ship's bell; an old man with an enormous white beard and striped turban bursting from the hold, shouting.

INTREPIDadj.fearless; adventurous🔊 Sound-hook: in-TREP-id ≈ “IN-TRIP-ID”: always ready for a trip, fearless
STORY

LINK

50→51 · The fearless adventurer (intrepid) meets a HYPER-BOLE: a hyper bowl of cereal that claims it's “the size of a mountain, the best in the UNIVERSE, EVER!” (Substitution: hy-PER-bo-le ≈ hyper-bowl-y; Exaggeration).

LOCI

Station 10 · TOP. At the tip of the tallest mast, an explorer stands on one foot with a telescope, fearless = INTREPID.
