# Chapter 12 · Mills of Midnight — Image prompts
📍 The Water-Mill Barges · Words 111–120

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #111 PERPETUAL (4:3)
A row of floating mill barges with huge wooden waterwheels turning endlessly in a moonlit river; kids and an old sailor arriving with a cracking egg.

### Frame 2 · #112 MONOTONOUS (4:3)
A yawning 10-year-old boy in a green cap leaning on a sack in a flour-dusted mill, bored; the wheel turning in the background.

### Frame 3 · #113 INDUSTRIOUS (4:3)
Hard-working millers in flour-dusted clothes hauling sacks and fixing paddles; one offering warm bread to three kids; an old sailor stuffing his face.

### Frame 4 · #114 TEDIOUS (4:3)
Three kids sitting cross-legged sorting a huge pile of beans one by one, heads drooping in boredom; a small ink djinn asleep.

### Frame 5 · #115 LABORIOUS (4:3)
Kids, millers and an old sailor straining together to push a giant stuck millstone, sweating; flour flying.

### Frame 6 · #116 NOCTURNAL (4:3)
Midnight on a mill barge; owls and bats circling; a giant speckled egg cracking open as an adorable fluffy sunset-red and gold baby roc bird pops out with half its eggshell on its head.

### Frame 7 · #117 CYCLICAL (4:3)
A delighted small boy hugging a fluffy baby roc chick; an old sailor explaining with a circling hand gesture; a turning waterwheel behind.

### Frame 8 · #118 EFFICIENT (4:3)
A girl scholar releasing a rolling millstone that flattens three shadow creatures in one path; an ink djinn cheering.

### Frame 9 · #119 STAGNANT (4:3)
A still, scummy green backwater behind floating mills where shadow creatures lurk under the surface; kids peering down from a balcony.

### Frame 0 · #120 HARNESS (wide 4:1)
A boy tying a rope from a turning waterwheel to a small boat, which pulls toward a domed floating bathhouse leaking steam; the baby roc riding on the boat.
