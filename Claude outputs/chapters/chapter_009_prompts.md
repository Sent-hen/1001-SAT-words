# Chapter 9 · The Bell That Couldn't Ring — Image prompts
📍 The Harbormaster's Tower · Words 81–90

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #81 TACITURN (4:3)
A grizzled old harbormaster sitting motionless at a tower door, arms folded, saying nothing; three kids and an old sailor trying to get an answer.

### Frame 2 · #82 RETICENT (4:3)
A kind girl scholar gently speaking to an old harbormaster who looks away, his eyes worried and guarded, holding something back.

### Frame 3 · #83 CACOPHONY (4:3)
Shadowy ink creatures banging gongs, pots and horns around the base of a harbor tower; kids covering their ears; jagged sound-effect lines.

### Frame 4 · #84 RESONATE (4:3)
An old sailor humming a gentle song; the old harbormaster's eyes filling with tears as warm golden music notes drift around him.

### Frame 5 · #85 DORMANT (4:3)
An old harbormaster pointing upward, explaining; inset of a bronze bell filled with black ink, sleeping, with tiny Zzz bubbles.

### Frame 6 · #86 REVERBERATE (4:3)
A small boy shouting up a tall stone stairwell with echo rings spiralling up; his friends grinning at the effect.

### Frame 7 · #87 STIFLE (4:3)
Shadow creatures draped like heavy blankets over three kids on a narrow stair, muffling them; a boy struggling to shout, his speech bubble tiny and squashed.

### Frame 8 · #88 INAUDIBLE (4:3)
A girl scholar screaming with an empty speech bubble while ink swallows the sound; a small ink djinn with glowing eyes reading her lips.

### Frame 9 · #89 CLAMOR (4:3)
A small ink djinn shaped like a giant scrubbing brush cleaning black ink from a bronze bell; below, a crowd of harbor people shouting and waving.

### Frame 0 · #90 SONOROUS (wide 4:1)
A boy pulling a bell rope as a huge bronze bell booms deeply, golden sound waves rolling over a harbor; shadow creatures shattering like black glass; a giant iron chain across the river in the distance.
