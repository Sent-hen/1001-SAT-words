# Chapter 5 · Skeleton Ships — Image prompts
📍 The Shipyard · Words 41–50

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #41 DILAPIDATED (4:3)
A moonlit shipyard full of half-built dhow skeletons on stilts like whale ribs, rotten planks, a sign hanging by one nail; three kids and a small ink djinn entering warily.

### Frame 2 · #42 EERIE (4:3)
Wind whistling through skeletal ship hulls at night, a lantern swinging by itself, a small frightened boy in a green cap clutching his older brother; blue-grey spooky lighting.

### Frame 3 · #43 RUDIMENTARY (4:3)
A boy proudly holding up a crude weapon made of a stick with a nail; a girl scholar sighing and rolling her eyes; shipyard sawdust and tools around.

### Frame 4 · #44 PRECARIOUS (4:3)
A small boy in a green cap standing on top of a wobbly, swaying stack of barrels in a moonlit shipyard; his brother below reaching up in panic.

### Frame 5 · #45 DERELICT (4:3)
A huge abandoned dhow ship with tattered sails and cobwebs, its brass name plate reading nothing legible; three kids staring up; faint snoring bubbles drifting from a porthole.

### Frame 6 · #46 CUMBERSOME (4:3)
Three kids straining to drag a huge awkward iron anchor across a shipyard floor, tangled in ropes; comic effort sweat drops.

### Frame 7 · #47 FORTIFY (4:3)
Kids hammering planks over holes in an old ship's hull while shadow creatures press in; an ink djinn stretched across a porthole like a shield.

### Frame 8 · #48 MAKESHIFT (4:3)
Three kids in comic makeshift armor: a bucket helmet, a frying-pan shield, a broom spear; standing bravely in a ship's hold.

### Frame 9 · #49 FORMIDABLE (4:3)
A giant, towering shadow creature bursting out of a ship's hatch, three times bigger than the others, dripping black ink; three tiny kids in makeshift armor looking up in fear.

### Frame 0 · #50 INTREPID (wide 4:1)
A boy in a bucket helmet fearlessly charging a giant shadow creature and ringing a ship's bell; an old man with an enormous white beard and striped turban bursting from the hold, shouting.
