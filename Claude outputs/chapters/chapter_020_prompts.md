# Chapter 20 · Toll of the Traitor — Image prompts
📍 The Toll Post (Bridge of Boats, West End) · Words 191–200

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #191 BETRAY (4:3)
A girl's heron-feather cloak rippling into violet sorcerer robes, a silver crescent pendant glowing; she holds a glowing satchel while the kids stare in shock.

### Frame 2 · #192 TREACHEROUS (4:3)
A rickety toll-post where planks flip open into the river under kids' feet; a boy leaping between safe boards.

### Frame 3 · #193 DUPLICITY (4:3)
A furious girl scholar confronting the sorceress-girl, a split image behind her showing the friendly Yasmin and the cold Mirage side by side.

### Frame 4 · #194 PERFIDIOUS (4:3)
An old sailor shaking his cane in outrage; the sorceress-girl shrugging coolly.

### Frame 5 · #195 DECEIT (4:3)
The sorceress-girl opening a glowing satchel as forty golden sparks stream into her crescent pendant; kids reaching out helplessly.

### Frame 6 · #196 GULLIBLE (4:3)
A small boy in a green cap in tears; his older brother with a sick, guilty look; a girl scholar clenching her fists.

### Frame 7 · #197 NAIVE (4:3)
A sorceress-girl kneeling beside a crying small boy, her face briefly soft and sad; a girl scholar noticing from the side.

### Frame 8 · #198 VINDICTIVE (4:3)
An enraged boy shouting after the sorceress-girl while a girl scholar holds his arm back; shadow creatures restraining an old sailor.

### Frame 9 · #199 ULTIMATUM (4:3)
The sorceress-girl pointing west toward a distant bazaar, issuing a threat; then transforming into a white heron and flying off.

### Frame 0 · #200 DISILLUSIONED (wide 4:1)
Three dejected kids sitting on the last plank of a bridge at sunrise; a boy with an orange headband standing up with determination; across the river a huge copper arch glinting.
