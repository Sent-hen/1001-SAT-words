# Chapter 45 · Counterfeit Kingdom — Image prompts
📍 The Mint · Words 441–450

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #441 COUNTERFEIT (4:3)
A mint with huge coin presses stamping out coins bearing a mouthless mask; piles of fake gold coins; kids sneaking in.

### Frame 2 · #442 AUTHENTIC (4:3)
A girl scholar biting two coins to compare them, one real gold and one painted brass; kids watching.

### Frame 3 · #443 SPURIOUS (4:3)
A girl scholar tracing through a ledger of obviously fake numbers; crossed-out and invented figures.

### Frame 4 · #444 CURRENCY (4:3)
A girl in a crimson bandana explaining gravely; a vision of empty bakery shelves and hungry people.

### Frame 5 · #445 FISCAL (4:3)
A trembling Mint Master confessing to the kids; a shadowy image of clockwork soldiers behind him.

### Frame 6 · #446 DEBASE (4:3)
A furnace mixing brass into molten gold, coins losing their shine; Hushlings stirring the mix.

### Frame 7 · #447 HOARD (4:3)
A secret vault revealed behind a false wall, filled with real gold and a crate of glowing sparks; kids gasping.

### Frame 8 · #448 SQUANDER (4:3)
A boy greedily grabbing at gold; a girl scholar stopping his hand firmly; a crate of sparks glowing.

### Frame 9 · #449 AUSTERITY (4:3)
A captain announcing strict rationing; plain bread on tables; a comic image of a round sweets merchant fainting in thought bubble.

### Frame 0 · #450 IMPOVERISHED (wide 4:1)
Kids handing gold to poor families in patched clothes; color flooding the streets; next door a bathhouse pouring out thick fog.
