# Chapter 31 · Over the Sarat — Image prompts
📍 The Sarat Canal Bridge · Words 301–310

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #301 MELANCHOLY (4:3)
A humpbacked stone bridge over a quiet canal under weeping willows at night; a boy with an orange headband sitting alone staring sadly at the water.

### Frame 2 · #302 SOMBER (4:3)
A gloomy group of kids, an old sailor and a baby roc sitting in silence on a canal bank; a small ink djinn turning grey at its edges.

### Frame 3 · #303 SOLACE (4:3)
A fluffy baby roc laying its head on a sad boy's lap; the boy gently stroking its feathers; warm glow.

### Frame 4 · #304 CONSOLE (4:3)
A girl scholar in tears beside a boy who snaps at her, then softens and puts a hand on her shoulder to comfort her.

### Frame 5 · #305 LAMENT (4:3)
An old sailor singing a mournful song with eyes closed; kids listening quietly; willow branches swaying.

### Frame 6 · #306 SERENE (4:3)
A perfectly calm moonlit canal with fireflies drifting like floating stars; willows reflected in glassy water.

### Frame 7 · #307 TRANQUIL (4:3)
A girl scholar teaching a boy to read by lantern light on a quiet bridge; the boy sounding out words with his finger on the page.

### Frame 8 · #308 PLACID (4:3)
A girl in a crimson bandana sitting by calm water, speaking quietly to a boy with an orange headband; her face calm but sad.

### Frame 9 · #309 WISTFUL (4:3)
A boy gazing longingly at distant round city walls in moonlight; a faint memory image of two brothers flying kites on rooftops.

### Frame 0 · #310 FORLORN (wide 4:1)
A lonely grey gardener sitting under a willow with dead roses; a girl scholar kneeling beside him; a great waterwheel creaking in the background.
