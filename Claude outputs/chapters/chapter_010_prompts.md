# Chapter 10 · Breaking the Chain — Image prompts
📍 The Chain Gate · Words 91–100

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #91 EMANCIPATE (4:3)
Two stone towers across a river holding a massive iron chain that blocks trapped dhows; grey, chained sailors on deck; three kids and an old sailor looking up with determination.

### Frame 2 · #92 FETTER (4:3)
Close-up of an enormous iron chain, each link bigger than a cart, stretched across a river; a small boy dwarfed beside a single link.

### Frame 3 · #93 IMPEDE (4:3)
Three giant shadow creatures and a pile of barrels and broken carts blocking the door of a winch tower; kids planning an approach.

### Frame 4 · #94 TRIUMPH (4:3)
An old sailor waving his whale-bone cane in a war-cry, a boy swinging from a tower rope, a small boy rolling a barrel into shadow creatures; heroic celebratory pose.

### Frame 5 · #95 EXULT (4:3)
Three kids and an old sailor cheering and hugging in a tower winch room; an old harbormaster throwing his hat in the air; a jammed iron drum behind them.

### Frame 6 · #96 MOMENTUM (4:3)
Kids and an old sailor pushing a large dhow down a wooden slipway; the ship starting to slide faster toward the river, sparks flying from the rails.

### Frame 7 · #97 CATALYST (4:3)
A small ink djinn diving into a ship's sail and transforming into a gust of golden wind; the ship surging forward.

### Frame 8 · #98 DEFIANCE (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband standing firm, fists raised, in front of a giant shadow creature at a lever atop a tower; the creature hesitating.

### Frame 9 · #99 UNYIELDING (4:3)
A dhow crashing hard into a massive iron chain across a river; the chain straining but holding; kids and an old sailor bracing on deck.

### Frame 0 · #100 CULMINATE (wide 4:1)
Climactic splash panel: an ink djinn shaped like a giant bouncing ball smashing through a massive iron chain which shatters; a dhow sailing free; the whole harbor flooding with color, cheering sailors, dawn breaking.
