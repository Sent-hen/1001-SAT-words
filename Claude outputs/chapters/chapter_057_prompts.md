# Chapter 57 · Ink of the First Night — Image prompts
📍 The Ink-Makers' Vats · Words 561–570

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #561 PRIMORDIAL (4:3)
Huge vats of bubbling black ink in a soot-blackened factory; black bubbles rising and popping; a small ink djinn trembling at the edge.

### Frame 2 · #562 GENESIS (4:3)
A small ink djinn touching the ink, and swirling memories rising: a shattered crystal book spilling ink that forms into shadow creatures.

### Frame 3 · #563 INCEPTION (4:3)
A memory vision in the ink: a thin 15-year-old boy practicing a poem nervously beside ink vats where his father works.

### Frame 4 · #564 NASCENT (4:3)
Memory vision: a boy writing a beautiful poem that glows on paper; a small sprout of golden light.

### Frame 5 · #565 EMBRYONIC (4:3)
Memory vision: a stammering boy on a poetry café stage, words stuck, the crowd laughing cruelly; the boy's face crumbling.

### Frame 6 · #566 ARCHETYPE (4:3)
A girl scholar and a boy with sad understanding in their eyes; faint ghostly images of many lonely kids behind the young Idris.

### Frame 7 · #567 PROTOTYPE (4:3)
Memory vision: a young boy shaping the very first small shadow creature from ink in the dark, tears on his face.

### Frame 8 · #568 INDIGENOUS (4:3)
Kids realizing the truth; a girl scholar holding up her hand as the shadow creatures in the vats waver.

### Frame 9 · #569 INNATE (4:3)
A small ink djinn speaking wisely, glowing gold at the edges; the kids listening deeply.

### Frame 0 · #570 INHERENT (wide 4:1)
Kids sealing ink vats with lids; shadow creatures shrinking; then ink boiling over and bursting toward pottery kilns.
