# Chapter 44 · The Healer's Gamble — Image prompts
📍 The Bimaristan (Hospital) · Words 431–440

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #431 REMEDY (4:3)
A grand medieval hospital courtyard with a fountain and herb gardens; a determined female physician with a notebook among patients turning grey.

### Frame 2 · #432 AILMENT (4:3)
Hospital ward with patients' faces turning grey; a boy with an orange headband looking at his own fingertips going pale.

### Frame 3 · #433 MALADY (4:3)
A physician pointing at a chart showing grey spreading with silence; patients silent and fading.

### Frame 4 · #434 BENIGN (4:3)
A girl scholar reading gently to a grey patient whose color slowly returns; the physician smiling.

### Frame 5 · #435 LETHAL (4:3)
An old guard almost completely grey on a hospital bed; a physician looking grave, holding an hourglass.

### Frame 6 · #436 ANTIDOTE (4:3)
A boy handing over a glowing spark to a physician mixing honey in a bowl; a hymn's musical notes swirling.

### Frame 7 · #437 CONTAGIOUS (4:3)
An old guard laughing as color floods back, and laughter spreading bed to bed through the ward like a wave; kids joining.

### Frame 8 · #438 ALLEVIATE (4:3)
Kids helping a physician pour herbal tea for patients; a baby roc comically spilling a cup.

### Frame 9 · #439 MITIGATE (4:3)
Shadow creatures shrinking and weakening at the hospital gates as laughter rings out from inside.

### Frame 0 · #440 CONVALESCE (wide 4:1)
Recovering patients resting peacefully in a sunny rooftop herb garden; a captain thanking the kids and pointing toward a building with coin presses.
