# Chapter 13 · Steam and Secrets — Image prompts
📍 The Floating Bathhouse · Words 121–130

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #121 CLANDESTINE (4:3)
A domed floating bathhouse full of steam; hooded figures in violet cloaks huddled in a secret meeting; three kids and an ink djinn hiding behind a tiled pillar.

### Frame 2 · #122 COVERT (4:3)
A comically disguised agent in a fake beard and bath towel whispering to hooded figures in steam; hidden kids watching.

### Frame 3 · #123 EAVESDROP (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband hanging upside-down from a ceiling beam above a steamy pool, secretly listening to hooded figures below.

### Frame 4 · #124 SURREPTITIOUS (4:3)
A girl scholar in an indigo headscarf quietly writing in a small notebook while crouched in steam; hooded figures unaware.

### Frame 5 · #125 CONSPIRE (4:3)
Hooded figures bowing before a mirror showing a mouthless silver mask glowing violet; steam swirling; sinister mood.

### Frame 6 · #126 OBSCURE (4:3)
Thick white steam hiding everything in a bathhouse; a shadowy figure creeping toward the kids' hiding place.

### Frame 7 · #127 DIVULGE (4:3)
A hooded agent grabbing a small boy in a green cap and demanding answers; a sneezing baby roc giving away their position; steam everywhere.

### Frame 8 · #128 INSINUATE (4:3)
A sly hooded agent whispering into a small boy's ear with a smirk, the boy looking hurt and doubtful; friends in the background.

### Frame 9 · #129 FURTIVE (4:3)
A boy creeping sneakily along a balcony above a steam pool, then dropping a fluffy baby roc onto a hooded agent's head; a small boy breaking free.

### Frame 0 · #130 DISCREET (wide 4:1)
Kids and an ink djinn escaping onto a domed roof, a girl scholar putting a finger to her lips; in the distance on a reedy island, a mysterious girl in a white heron-feather cloak watching.
