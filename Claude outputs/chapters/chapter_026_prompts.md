# Chapter 26 · Hammer and Echo — Image prompts
📍 Coppersmiths' Lane · Words 251–260

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #251 MALLEABLE (4:3)
A lane of coppersmiths with copper pots hanging everywhere; a burly master smith bending glowing hot copper like dough in front of amazed kids.

### Frame 2 · #252 STRIDENT (4:3)
A burly coppersmith yelling orders with a booming voice, jagged sound lines; a small boy covering his ears.

### Frame 3 · #253 DISCORDANT (4:3)
Dozens of coppersmiths hammering out of rhythm, clashing musical notes flying in all directions; a baby roc squawking along.

### Frame 4 · #254 VIGOROUS (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband hammering a copper sheet with energetic strokes, sparks flying; a coppersmith nodding approval.

### Frame 5 · #255 RAUCOUS (4:3)
Laughing coppersmiths in a rowdy contest banging pots with all their might; kids joining in joyfully.

### Frame 6 · #256 INCESSANT (4:3)
A girl scholar shouting letters at a boy who squints at a slate while coppersmiths bang endlessly behind them.

### Frame 7 · #257 DIN (4:3)
A shadowy spy slipping into a noisy copper workshop; a small ink djinn with glowing eyes noticing from a shelf.

### Frame 8 · #258 TEMPER (4:3)
A burly coppersmith plunging a glowing copper satchel into a water trough with a huge hiss of steam.

### Frame 9 · #259 DUCTILE (4:3)
A small ink djinn stretching a strand of copper into a long thin wire that trips a sneaking spy; kids pointing.

### Frame 0 · #260 ROBUST (wide 4:1)
A finished gleaming copper satchel identical to a glowing leather one; a girl in a crimson bandana grinning; a caught spy tied up in copper wire.
