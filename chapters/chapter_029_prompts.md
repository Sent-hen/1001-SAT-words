# Chapter 29 · The Mirror Maze — Image prompts
📍 The Hall of Mirrors · Words 281–290

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #281 ILLUSION (4:3)
A dark hall of mirrors glowing with a thousand candle reflections, only one candle real; three kids entering, surrounded by their reflections.

### Frame 2 · #282 DISTORT (4:3)
Funhouse-mirror reflections of kids stretched wide, tall and with multiple heads; the kids laughing, then freezing as the reflections move on their own.

### Frame 3 · #283 INTROSPECTIVE (4:3)
A boy facing his own mirror reflection, which speaks to him with a cruel smirk; the boy looking down thoughtfully.

### Frame 4 · #284 NARCISSISTIC (4:3)
A sorceress-girl in a feather cloak reflected in dozens of mirrors, admiring herself; a girl scholar scoffing.

### Frame 5 · #285 PARADOX (4:3)
A girl scholar closing her eyes in a hall of mirrors, sensing her way; a riddle written in glowing letters around her.

### Frame 6 · #286 FACSIMILE (4:3)
A girl scholar with eyes closed pointing confidently at one reflection among many identical copies; the real sorceress-girl slowly clapping.

### Frame 7 · #287 REPLICATE (4:3)
A sorceress-girl conjuring twenty identical copper satchels in a circle around her; kids confused.

### Frame 8 · #288 DELUSION (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband staring defiantly at the sorceress-girl; she flinches, a crack appearing in one mirror.

### Frame 9 · #289 PERCEPTION (4:3)
Mirrors cracking all around, revealing a plain shabby room with bent glass; kids blinking in the plain light.

### Frame 0 · #290 VANITY (wide 4:1)
A sorceress-girl pausing to fix her hair in a mirror while a girl in a crimson bandana snatches a satchel from beside her; then the sorceress vanishing in a shimmer.
