# Chapter 49 · Echoes in the Cistern — Image prompts
📍 The Underground Cistern · Words 481–490

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #481 CAVERNOUS (4:3)
A vast underground cistern with hundreds of stone columns standing in dark water, lit by glowing fungus; tiny kids and a horse-sized roc at the entrance.

### Frame 2 · #482 SUBTERRANEAN (4:3)
Underground tunnels branching off a cistern; pale blind fish in dark water; kids exploring by glowing light.

### Frame 3 · #483 DANK (4:3)
Cold drips falling from a vaulted ceiling; a girl scholar shivering, wrapping her scarf tighter.

### Frame 4 · #484 DESOLATE (4:3)
An empty, bleak, silent section of the cistern; dark water, no life; kids moving quietly.

### Frame 5 · #485 SECLUDED (4:3)
A hidden chamber behind an underground waterfall; an old woman sitting alone by a candle.

### Frame 6 · #486 ISOLATE (4:3)
An old woman explaining with images of people trapped on separate islands, fading; versus people holding hands, glowing.

### Frame 7 · #487 SOLITUDE (4:3)
An old woman happily chatting for a long time; kids listening politely; a horse-sized roc asleep.

### Frame 8 · #488 RECLUSE (4:3)
An old woman revealing a small portrait of a silver-bearded astronomer; the kids amazed.

### Frame 9 · #489 SANCTUARY (4:3)
A cozy hidden chamber whose walls are covered floor to ceiling with handwritten words; shadow creatures unable to enter.

### Frame 0 · #490 REFUGE (wide 4:1)
An old woman handing a map to kids in the morning; from above, the sound of battle drums; kids looking up.
