# Chapter 36 · Tears of Roses — Image prompts
📍 The Rosewater Distillery · Words 351–360

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #351 DISTILL (4:3)
A distillery full of bubbling copper stills; mountains of rose petals; a single glowing pink drop falling into a small bottle; kids watching.

### Frame 2 · #352 ESSENCE (4:3)
A kind old distiller handing a tiny glowing bottle to a girl scholar; above each kid, a small glowing symbol of their core nature.

### Frame 3 · #353 POIGNANT (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband with tears in his eyes, telling his friends something painful; memory images of his brother and mother in the steam.

### Frame 4 · #354 SENTIMENTAL (4:3)
An old sailor gently holding a dried flower, eyes soft; kids smiling kindly.

### Frame 5 · #355 NOSTALGIA (4:3)
A girl in a crimson bandana smelling jasmine, eyes closed; a faint image of a childhood garden around her.

### Frame 6 · #356 EVOKE (4:3)
A small ink djinn shaping rising steam into images of memories: a laughing small boy, a grandmother reading, a jasmine garden.

### Frame 7 · #357 EMPATHY (4:3)
A boy looking at a girl scholar with sudden understanding; the steam shows her standing alone in shadow.

### Frame 8 · #358 COMPASSION (4:3)
A boy hugging a girl scholar warmly; friends smiling; pink steam swirling like a heart.

### Frame 9 · #359 VULNERABLE (4:3)
A girl scholar admitting her fear quietly; a boy grinning reassuringly; a soft emotional moment.

### Frame 0 · #360 CATHARSIS (wide 4:1)
Kids laughing and crying together in a pink steamy distillery; the mood lifting; through a window a pomegranate orchard glowing red at dawn.
