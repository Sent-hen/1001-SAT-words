# Chapter 32 · The Wheel of Fortune — Image prompts
📍 The Great Waterwheel · Words 311–320

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #311 FORTUITOUS (4:3)
A giant wooden waterwheel lifting buckets; one bucket tipping and dropping a glowing spark straight into a boy's hand; surprised faces.

### Frame 2 · #312 SERENDIPITY (4:3)
A girl scholar tripping over a root and finding a hidden bucket of glowing sparks; everyone laughing in surprise.

### Frame 3 · #313 INEVITABLE (4:3)
An old grey gardener waking slightly, gesturing toward the turning wheel as he speaks wisely; kids listening.

### Frame 4 · #314 ADVERSITY (4:3)
Montage of past hardships in the waterwheel's buckets: fire, betrayal, a stolen brother; the kids reflecting.

### Frame 5 · #315 PROSPERITY (4:3)
A girl scholar handing a glowing spark to an old gardener whose dead roses burst into red and gold bloom; color returning.

### Frame 6 · #316 VICISSITUDE (4:3)
A celebration interrupted: the waterwheel jamming with a loud crack and a shadow creature crawling out of a bucket.

### Frame 7 · #317 FICKLE (4:3)
A shadow creature fleeing with a spark as a swinging bucket smacks it into the canal; kids cheering in surprise.

### Frame 8 · #318 HAPHAZARD (4:3)
A boy wedging a random assortment of objects (a shoe, a stick, a turnip) into the gears of a jammed waterwheel; it somehow works.

### Frame 9 · #319 PROVIDENTIAL (4:3)
A gust of wind freeing the waterwheel and lifting a fluffy baby roc into its first real flight, flapping joyfully ten feet in the air.

### Frame 0 · #320 WANE (wide 4:1)
A shrinking moon over a garden; an old gardener pointing toward a walled rose garden where roses have tiny faces.
