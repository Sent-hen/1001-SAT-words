# Chapter 18 · Belly of the Tanin — Image prompts
📍 The Tanin's Whirlpool · Words 171–180

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #171 VORACIOUS (4:3)
A colossal ancient teal river serpent with barnacled scales, catfish whiskers and huge yellow eyes rising from a whirlpool with its jaws wide open; tiny kids in a bubble below.

### Frame 2 · #172 ENGULF (4:3)
A giant serpent's mouth closing around a tiny air bubble containing kids; total darkness closing in.

### Frame 3 · #173 COLOSSAL (4:3)
Inside a gigantic serpent stomach the size of a stadium, lit by eerie glowing glands; three swallowed ships, a camel and a waving fisherman; kids gaping.

### Frame 4 · #174 CLAUSTROPHOBIC (4:3)
Kids crawling through a narrow, tight fleshy tunnel inside a serpent, a small boy panicking, walls pressing close.

### Frame 5 · #175 REVULSION (4:3)
A boy face-first in green slime inside a serpent; a girl scholar gagging with disgust; exaggerated comic expressions.

### Frame 6 · #176 LOATHE (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband scowling while wiping slime off; a small ink djinn turned sickly green.

### Frame 7 · #177 DESPERATE (4:3)
Churning, bubbling stomach acid rising around kids clinging to a swallowed ship's deck; frantic faces.

### Frame 8 · #178 IMPROVISE (4:3)
An old sailor and kids frantically tickling the walls of a serpent's stomach with big red-gold feathers; a baby roc squawking.

### Frame 9 · #179 EXPEL (4:3)
Kids, an old sailor and a baby roc shooting out of a giant serpent's blowhole in a geyser of water into the night sky.

### Frame 0 · #180 GROTESQUE (wide 4:1)
Moonlit silhouette of a colossal warty, whiskered serpent face, strangely sad, as kids fly through the air toward a maze of tall reeds.
