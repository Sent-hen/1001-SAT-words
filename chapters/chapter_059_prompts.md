# Chapter 59 · Colors of Rebellion — Image prompts
📍 The Dyers' Pools · Words 581–590

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #581 DISSENT (4:3)
Dyers' pools in rainbow colors; workers in stained aprons refusing to dye cloth grey, holding up colored cloth defiantly.

### Frame 2 · #582 INSURGENT (4:3)
A tall dyer with blue-stained arms leading workers, raising a fist; colorful cloth waving like flags.

### Frame 3 · #583 MUTINY (4:3)
Dyers throwing buckets of bright dye at shadow creature guards who become colorful and confused.

### Frame 4 · #584 RADICAL (4:3)
Shadow creatures splashed with bright dye shrinking and weakening; a girl scholar scribbling excitedly.

### Frame 5 · #585 UPHEAVAL (4:3)
A whole quarter erupting in a battle of flying color; grey people bursting into bright hues; shadow creatures fleeing.

### Frame 6 · #586 VIVID (4:3)
Streets glowing in vivid crimson, indigo, saffron and emerald; a large roc dyed bright purple, looking confused.

### Frame 7 · #587 GARISH (4:3)
An old sailor proudly wearing a garish neon orange, pink and green turban; the kids cringing and laughing.

### Frame 8 · #588 ICONOCLAST (4:3)
A tall dyer tearing down a giant grey banner showing a mouthless mask; the crowd cheering.

### Frame 9 · #589 NONCONFORMIST (4:3)
A boy looking at a diverse colorful crowd of rebels, each unique; a realization dawning.

### Frame 0 · #590 PROVOCATIVE (wide 4:1)
A mural of a mouthless mask with a big painted smile; far away a giant scroll-shaped shadow shrieking in rage; smoke rising from a calligraphy school.
