# Chapter 50 · Sinbad Falls — Image prompts
📍 The Southeast Watchtower · Words 491–500

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #491 VALIANT (4:3)
An old sailor bravely swinging his whale-bone cane at a three-story iron automaton at the base of a tall watchtower.

### Frame 2 · #492 ALTRUISTIC (4:3)
An old sailor shouting at the kids to run; kids reaching toward him; the automaton looming.

### Frame 3 · #493 SELFLESS (4:3)
A horse-sized red-and-gold roc refusing to leave an old sailor's side, spreading its wings protectively.

### Frame 4 · #494 GRIEVOUS (4:3)
A giant iron fist striking the watchtower; a massive crack; an old sailor staggering.

### Frame 5 · #495 MOURN (4:3)
A girl scholar with tears watching the watchtower lean; hands over her mouth.

### Frame 6 · #496 STALWART (4:3)
An old sailor wrapping a heavy chain around the legs of a giant automaton; determined face.

### Frame 7 · #497 MAGNANIMOUS (4:3)
An old sailor gently telling a small shadow creature to go home, kindly, amid the battle.

### Frame 8 · #498 BEREFT (4:3)
A watchtower collapsing into a river, an old sailor and a giant automaton falling; a horse-sized roc screaming; kids devastated.

### Frame 9 · #499 RETRIBUTION (4:3)
A furious boy wanting revenge; a girl scholar gripping his arm; a girl in a crimson bandana pointing at dark boats in the river.

### Frame 0 · #500 CAPTIVE (wide 4:1)
An old sailor in chains being dragged by shadow creatures in dark boats toward a city gate with a giant brass elephant; three kids and a large roc watching from the ruins at sunset.
