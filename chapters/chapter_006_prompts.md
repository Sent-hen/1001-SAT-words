# Chapter 6 · The Liar Who Never Lied — Image prompts
📍 Sinbad's Ship, the Wandering Pearl · Words 51–60

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #51 HYPERBOLE (4:3)
An old legendary sailor with an enormous white beard braided into anchor shapes and a red-and-white striped turban, arms flung wide telling a tall tale on the deck of a beached ship; three kids and an ink djinn listening skeptically.

### Frame 2 · #52 EMBELLISH (4:3)
An old sailor gesturing dramatically while above his head a thought bubble shows a sea-serpent gaining more and more decorations: crown, jewels, a singing mouth; kids exchanging amused looks.

### Frame 3 · #53 RACONTEUR (4:3)
An old sailor telling a story by lamplight on a ship's deck; his hands painting glowing images in the air; kids, an ink djinn and even shadowy creatures at the rail leaning in, spellbound.

### Frame 4 · #54 CREDULOUS (4:3)
A wide-eyed 10-year-old boy in a green cap hanging on an old sailor's every word, stars in his eyes; his older brother nudging him with a skeptical look.

### Frame 5 · #55 VERACITY (4:3)
A giant speckled red-and-gold egg the size of a donkey resting in a nest of old sails on a ship's deck; the old sailor proudly gesturing at it; stunned kids.

### Frame 6 · #56 FABRICATE (4:3)
An old sailor winking at a small boy while a fabric monster stitches fake stories behind him; comic cutaway, warm lantern light.

### Frame 7 · #57 FLAMBOYANT (4:3)
An old sailor in a flamboyant peacock-feather hat and silk cape posing dramatically at a cabin window with seven colorful flags; kids laughing.

### Frame 8 · #58 BRAGGART (4:3)
An old sailor boasting with thumbs pointed at himself, puffing out his chest; a boy with an orange headband rolling his eyes; a girl scholar hiding a smile.

### Frame 9 · #59 ANECDOTE (4:3)
A quiet moment on a moonlit ship balcony; an old sailor gently telling a short personal story to three kids; a faint image of his younger self in the air.

### Frame 0 · #60 GRANDIOSE (wide 4:1)
An old sailor on a crow's nest raising his cane with grand imaginary banners behind him, while below the giant egg cracks and ink-shadow creatures climb over the ship rail.
