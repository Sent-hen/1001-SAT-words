# Chapter 100 · The Thousand and First Word — Image prompts
📍 The Crown of Stars · Words 991–1000

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #991 DENOUEMENT (4:3)
A thousand golden sparks flying home into a crystal book that re-forms whole and blazing above a green dome; a joyful city below in full color.

### Frame 2 · #992 EPILOGUE (4:3)
A rebuilt House of Wisdom full of students; an old astronomer teaching with his sister.

### Frame 3 · #993 CONSUMMATE (4:3)
An old sailor telling a story to a big crowd at a harbor, with a full-grown giant roc perched behind him.

### Frame 4 · #994 QUINTESSENTIAL (4:3)
A girl in a crimson bandana teaching a lively school of orphan kids; a small boy in a green cap talking nonstop.

### Frame 5 · #995 INEFFABLE (4:3)
A girl scholar writing with a golden-and-black ink pen that glows; a small happy face in the ink.

### Frame 6 · #996 PERPETUATE (4:3)
A girl scholar and a feathered girl telling a story together to a crowd at night in a lantern-lit square.

### Frame 7 · #997 GRATITUDE (4:3)
A thin gentle man writing a heartfelt thank-you letter at a desk, sunlight streaming in.

### Frame 8 · #998 HUMILITY (4:3)
A gentle man teaching a poetry class to children; a small child stammering while everyone listens kindly.

### Frame 9 · #999 ENDURING (4:3)
A boy and a girl scholar leaping off the top of a green dome together on the back of a giant red-gold roc, laughing, the sunrise behind them.

### Frame 0 · #1000 TRANSCEND (wide 4:1)
A boy with an orange headband writing the last page of a glowing book at dawn; his written words floating up to become stars; at the bottom of the page, an empty speech bubble waiting for the reader.
