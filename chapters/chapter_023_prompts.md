# Chapter 23 · The Carpet That Ran Away — Image prompts
📍 Carpet Weavers' Alley · Words 221–230

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #221 ERRATIC (4:3)
A flying carpet zigzagging wildly out of a narrow alley hung with colorful carpets; a small boy in a green cap and a fluffy baby roc clinging on; weavers shouting.

### Frame 2 · #222 CAPRICIOUS (4:3)
A flying carpet with an expressive face-like pattern suddenly bucking and spinning after floating calmly; a boy and baby roc tumbling.

### Frame 3 · #223 AGILE (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband leaping across rooftops, sliding under laundry lines and flipping off an awning in pursuit of a flying carpet.

### Frame 4 · #224 FRENETIC (4:3)
A wild, frenzied chase through a bazaar with weavers, orphan kids, barking dogs and a galloping camel all running after a flying carpet.

### Frame 5 · #225 WAYWARD (4:3)
A wayward flying carpet swerving off course into an alley lined with crystal perfume bottles; weavers facepalming.

### Frame 6 · #226 UNRULY (4:3)
A flying carpet with whipping tassels knocking off hats; a flapping baby roc making chaos worse; bottles and scarves flying.

### Frame 7 · #227 CAREEN (4:3)
A flying carpet careening sideways down a narrow street at high speed, narrowly missing a fruit cart, a fountain and an old man.

### Frame 8 · #228 PURSUE (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband and a girl in a crimson bandana sprinting side by side, chasing a flying carpet through a bazaar street.

### Frame 9 · #229 EXASPERATE (4:3)
A frustrated boy tearing at his hair while a flying carpet teasingly wiggles its tassels just out of reach, then zips away.

### Frame 0 · #230 NIMBLE (wide 4:1)
A girl in a crimson bandana running lightly along a clothesline and leaping onto a flying carpet to grab a small boy; the carpet diving toward a glittering cloud of perfume.
