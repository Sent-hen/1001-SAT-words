# Chapter 28 · A Thousand Wings — Image prompts
📍 The Bird Market · Words 271–280

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #271 MIGRATE (4:3)
A bird market with walls of stacked cages full of parrots, canaries, peacocks and pigeons; a small boy looking sadly at caged migrating birds.

### Frame 2 · #272 PLUMAGE (4:3)
A peacock spreading its magnificent shimmering tail fan; a glowing golden spark hidden among the eyespots; kids amazed.

### Frame 3 · #273 CAPTIVITY (4:3)
A greedy birdseller laughing and locking a peacock's cage; the glowing spark trapped inside.

### Frame 4 · #274 CONFINE (4:3)
Cages too small for the birds inside, wings crumpled; a baby roc shivering at the sight.

### Frame 5 · #275 SOAR (4:3)
A boy picking a cage lock; a peacock soaring into the blue sky with its tail streaming; a glowing spark falling into a girl's outstretched hand.

### Frame 6 · #276 UNFETTERED (4:3)
Orphan kids in grey feathers flinging open hundreds of cages as thousands of colorful birds burst into the sky.

### Frame 7 · #277 FLIT (4:3)
Canaries flitting around a laughing small boy's head like a golden crown; bright happy colors.

### Frame 8 · #278 FLOURISH (4:3)
A bird market bursting back into bright color; merchants happily selling birdseed and nests; flowers blooming on stalls.

### Frame 9 · #279 FLEDGLING (4:3)
A fluffy baby roc flapping clumsily, rising an inch and falling on its beak; an old sailor chuckling.

### Frame 0 · #280 CAGEY (wide 4:1)
A nervous birdseller avoiding eye contact and glancing toward a hall of mirrors across the square; a white heron landing on its roof.
