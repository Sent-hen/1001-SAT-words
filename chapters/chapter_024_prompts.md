# Chapter 24 · A Scent of Lies — Image prompts
📍 Perfumers' Row · Words 231–240

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #231 FRAGRANT (4:3)
A flying carpet crash-landing in an elegant street lined with shelves of crystal perfume bottles; colorful scented smoke; kids tumbling off smiling.

### Frame 2 · #232 AROMATIC (4:3)
A glamorous perfumer in golden robes holding out small glowing bottles of oil to the kids; curls of colored scented smoke.

### Frame 3 · #233 PERVASIVE (4:3)
Pink shimmering smoke creeping through every doorway and window of a perfume street; people nodding dreamily.

### Frame 4 · #234 SUBTLE (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband holding out his satchel to a smiling perfumer with a dazed look; faint pink smoke around his head.

### Frame 5 · #235 ALLURE (4:3)
Dazed shoppers lining up to hand glowing sparks to a glamorous perfumer who gathers them into a crystal bottle; charm sparkles.

### Frame 6 · #236 POTENT (4:3)
A girl scholar covering her nose with black ink-soaked cloth; a small boy swaying dizzily; strong pink smoke.

### Frame 7 · #237 CLOYING (4:3)
A baby roc and a boy gagging comically at an overwhelmingly sweet sticky pink smell; syrupy clouds.

### Frame 8 · #238 DECEPTIVE (4:3)
A girl scholar yanking at a perfumer's golden robe; a glimpse of violet underneath.

### Frame 9 · #239 FACADE (4:3)
A glamorous perfume shop revealed as a painted board facade; a mouthless shadow creature behind a painted mask; kids gasping.

### Frame 0 · #240 REDOLENT (wide 4:1)
A peaceful perfume street after the spell breaks; a small boy sniffing the air with a nostalgic smile; his older brother looking sad and quiet, thinking of their mother.
