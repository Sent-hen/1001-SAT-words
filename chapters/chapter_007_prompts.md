# Chapter 7 · Dive for the Drowned Spark — Image prompts
📍 The Pearl Divers' Pier · Words 61–70

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #61 SUBMERGE (4:3)
A boy with an orange headband diving off a moonlit pier into dark harbor water, a diving stone roped to his waist; a glowing golden spark sinking below; friends watching from the pier.

### Frame 2 · #62 BUOYANT (4:3)
A cheerful small ink djinn bobbing on a cork float on moonlit water, grinning; a girl on the pier holding out a rope.

### Frame 3 · #63 PERILOUS (4:3)
Underwater at night, a boy swimming carefully between sharp coral, snapping eels and a giant sleeping crab toward a glowing golden spark; tense, blue-green light.

### Frame 4 · #64 ABYSS (4:3)
A boy underwater slipping over a ledge into a vast bottomless dark abyss, a glowing golden spark falling below him into the black.

### Frame 5 · #65 LUMINOUS (4:3)
A deep underwater cavern suddenly lit up by thousands of glowing blue and gold shells; a boy in awe; a golden spark shining at the center.

### Frame 6 · #66 MURKY (4:3)
Underwater, black ink spreading and clouding the water; a boy squinting and groping blindly for a faint golden glow.

### Frame 7 · #67 ENDURANCE (4:3)
A struggling boy underwater, cheeks puffed, bubbles escaping, pushing onward through dark water; his hand finally closing on a warm golden spark.

### Frame 8 · #68 ASCEND (4:3)
A boy shooting up through water toward the moonlit surface in a column of bubbles; a girl and a small boy hauling on a rope from the pier above.

### Frame 9 · #69 RECKLESS (4:3)
A soaked girl scholar angrily scolding a dripping, grinning boy on a pier at night; he holds up a glowing spark; a little boy wrapping him in a blanket.

### Frame 0 · #70 EXHILARATING (wide 4:1)
A boy lifted into the air by a swirl of golden light above a pier, laughing with joy; in the distance, orange smoke rising from warehouses by the harbor.
