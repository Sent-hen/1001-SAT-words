# Chapter 53 · Glass Hearts — Image prompts
📍 The Glassblowers' Kiln · Words 521–530

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #521 TRANSLUCENT (4:3)
A glassblowers' workshop full of translucent glass bubbles and vases glowing in kiln light; kids landing on paper wings.

### Frame 2 · #522 SINCERE (4:3)
A friendly-looking master glassblower welcoming the kids warmly with open arms.

### Frame 3 · #523 EARNEST (4:3)
A glassblower working intently on a glass lantern; a girl scholar in the background noticing something suspicious.

### Frame 4 · #524 GENUINE (4:3)
Two shelves: real Baghdad glass glowing beautifully versus cheap copies stamped with a mouthless mask.

### Frame 5 · #525 PRETENSE (4:3)
A glassblower's smile slipping to reveal a contract with a mouthless mask seal behind his back.

### Frame 6 · #526 HYPOCRITE (4:3)
A boy pointing accusingly at a glassblower; the glassblower caught red-handed with a pouch of Vizier's coins.

### Frame 7 · #527 AFFECTATION (4:3)
A glassblower suddenly acting fake-posh, pinky finger raised, putting on airs; kids unimpressed.

### Frame 8 · #528 BRAZEN (4:3)
A glassblower shamelessly smashing a glass lantern and grabbing glowing sparks while shouting for guards.

### Frame 9 · #529 PROFESS (4:3)
A girl in a heron-feather cloak snatching glowing sparks from the corrupt glassblower and tossing them to a surprised girl scholar before vanishing.

### Frame 0 · #530 IMPECCABLE (wide 4:1)
A girl scholar catching sparks perfectly; puzzled kids looking at each other; stars rising over a workshop full of brass astrolabes.
