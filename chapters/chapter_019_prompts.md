# Chapter 19 · Lost in the Reeds — Image prompts
📍 The Reed Maze · Words 181–190

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #181 DISORIENTED (4:3)
Soaked kids in a maze of towering reeds taller than grown men, a boy spinning in confusion; moonlight and fireflies.

### Frame 2 · #182 LABYRINTHINE (4:3)
Aerial view of a vast maze of reed channels twisting and looping; tiny kids passing the same broken boat again.

### Frame 3 · #183 MEANDER (4:3)
A lazy stream winding like a snake through tall reeds; a small boy pointing along it; a girl in a heron-feather cloak shaking her head.

### Frame 4 · #184 CIRCUITOUS (4:3)
A girl in a heron-feather cloak leading the group on a winding, roundabout route through reeds; a girl scholar frowning at a map.

### Frame 5 · #185 BEWILDER (4:3)
Fireflies creating confusing false paths and shifting shadows in reeds; kids turning in confusion; a baby roc bumping into a reed.

### Frame 6 · #186 IMPENETRABLE (4:3)
A wall of reeds so thick that a boy's knife bends against it; kids facing a dead end.

### Frame 7 · #187 INTUITION (4:3)
A boy with his eyes closed and head tilted, listening; faint glowing sound waves of a river guiding him through reeds.

### Frame 8 · #188 PERSEVERANCE (4:3)
Exhausted kids wading and crawling through reeds, falling and getting up, finally glimpsing a lantern-lit toll gate at dawn.

### Frame 9 · #189 DENSE (4:3)
Thick white fog rolling over the reeds; kids barely visible; a small boy looking around; the girl in the feather cloak gone.

### Frame 0 · #190 ELUSIVE (wide 4:1)
Fog clearing to reveal a girl in a heron-feather cloak standing at a striped toll gate, holding a glowing leather satchel, smiling coldly.
