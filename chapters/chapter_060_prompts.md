# Chapter 60 · The Pen Reborn — Image prompts
📍 The Calligraphers' School · Words 591–600

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #591 REJUVENATE (4:3)
A calligraphy school with smoke and flames; a grey, fading ink djinn struggling; an old master calligrapher holding a golden pen nib.

### Frame 2 · #592 REVIVE (4:3)
Friends circling a fading ink djinn, each speaking a word that glows gold and flows into it, reviving it.

### Frame 3 · #593 RENAISSANCE (4:3)
An ink djinn reborn brilliantly: black ink shot through with shimmering gold, a golden pen nib crest shining; students gasping.

### Frame 4 · #594 RESURGENCE (4:3)
Students returning to long writing desks, ink flowing again; the fire dying down behind a golden-black djinn.

### Frame 5 · #595 INVIGORATE (4:3)
Kids and students energetically fighting a fire with buckets of ink-water; golden energy swirling around them.

### Frame 6 · #596 METAMORPHOSIS (4:3)
An ink djinn transforming briefly into a magnificent golden dragon made of calligraphy, then shrinking back.

### Frame 7 · #597 REDEMPTION (4:3)
A white heron landing at night and becoming a feathered girl; beside her an old sailor rubbing freed wrists; kids stunned.

### Frame 8 · #598 SALVAGE (4:3)
A feathered girl saying a quiet line and then flying away as a heron; a girl scholar watching thoughtfully.

### Frame 9 · #599 REHABILITATE (4:3)
An old sailor recovering in a garden, a large roc snuggled beside him; kids bringing him tea.

### Frame 0 · #600 AMEND (wide 4:1)
A girl scholar writing a new line in a glowing book; in the distance, a great city gate with a giant brass eagle spreading its wings.
