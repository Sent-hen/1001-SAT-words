# Chapter 30 · Sold to the Shadow — Image prompts
📍 The Auction Square · Words 291–300

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #291 COERCE (4:3)
A packed auction square; a sinister auctioneer in a black silk turban holding up a caged, gagged small boy in a green cap; kids in the crowd horrified.

### Frame 2 · #292 RUTHLESS (4:3)
A sinister auctioneer slicing open a cake to reveal a copper satchel, laughing coldly and dropping it into a fountain.

### Frame 3 · #293 DESPAIR (4:3)
Kids in a crowd with faces falling in despair; grey tones creeping over them; a small boy's cage in the background.

### Frame 4 · #294 ANGUISH (4:3)
A small boy in a cage crying out, reaching through the bars; his older brother's face twisted in anguish.

### Frame 5 · #295 ABDUCT (4:3)
Shadow creatures rising from a fountain; hooded men throwing a small boy into a black carriage; orphan kids in grey feathers charging too late.

### Frame 6 · #296 RANSOM (4:3)
A sinister auctioneer shouting a demand from the stage while a black carriage races away toward a distant round walled city with a green dome.

### Frame 7 · #297 CALLOUS (4:3)
A rich buyer in fine clothes stepping coldly over a fallen child in a scattering crowd; Marjana helping the child up.

### Frame 8 · #298 DESPOT (4:3)
A giant glowing image of a mouthless silver mask filling the sky above a bazaar square; a scroll unrolling with commanding words; people cowering.

### Frame 9 · #299 TYRANNY (4:3)
Shadow creatures swarming a bazaar, draining color from stalls; people falling silent and grey one by one.

### Frame 0 · #300 OPPRESS (wide 4:1)
A boy with an orange headband on his knees in a grey square; a girl scholar kneeling beside him with a hand on his shoulder; in the distance, moonlit gardens and a quiet canal.
