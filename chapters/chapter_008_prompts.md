# Chapter 8 · Pepper Storm — Image prompts
📍 The Spice Warehouses · Words 71–80

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #71 PUNGENT (4:3)
Three kids entering a dim brick spice warehouse full of sacks, eyes watering, a small boy sneezing violently; clouds of red and yellow spice dust in lantern light.

### Frame 2 · #72 VOLATILE (4:3)
Shadowy ink creatures knocking over barrels of lamp oil in a spice warehouse full of floating spice dust; a girl scholar holding out an arm to stop her friends.

### Frame 3 · #73 CHAOTIC (4:3)
A warehouse erupting in flame and flying spice dust, sacks bursting, kids scattering in different directions; chaotic comic motion lines.

### Frame 4 · #74 PANDEMONIUM (4:3)
A harbor at night in total uproar: bells clanging, dock workers shouting, a camel stampeding, a parrot screaming, orange fire glow over everything.

### Frame 5 · #75 ACRID (4:3)
Black bitter smoke curling through a burning warehouse; kids coughing, faces covered with torn cloth masks; eyes streaming.

### Frame 6 · #76 TORRENT (4:3)
A small ink djinn rising from a river as a gigantic wave with golden eyes, crashing through warehouse doors; burning oil swept aside; kids clinging to posts.

### Frame 7 · #77 INUNDATE (4:3)
A spice warehouse flooded to the rafters, three kids floating on bobbing spice sacks and cinnamon logs; a small ink djinn apologetic on top of a barrel.

### Frame 8 · #78 IGNITE (4:3)
A dripping shadow creature on a staircase flinging a stolen glowing spark at a barrel of oil, which bursts into flame; kids below shouting NO.

### Frame 9 · #79 CONFLAGRATION (4:3)
A row of spice warehouses consumed by a towering fire against the night sky, orange sparks and smoke; tiny silhouettes of kids and an old sailor running.

### Frame 0 · #80 HAVOC (wide 4:1)
Morning after a fire: a harbor strewn with burned sacks, broken carts and soot; exhausted kids covered in ash; in the background a tall square tower with a silent bronze bell.
