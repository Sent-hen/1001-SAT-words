# Chapter 34 · The Zoo of Silent Beasts — Image prompts
📍 The Caliph's Menagerie · Words 331–340

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #331 DOCILE (4:3)
A grand zoo of marble pens where animals have turned grey and silent; a gentle grey elephant letting a girl scholar touch its trunk.

### Frame 2 · #332 FEROCIOUS (4:3)
A shadow creature touching a lion that erupts into ferocity, snapping chains and charging; kids scattering.

### Frame 3 · #333 FERAL (4:3)
A zoo turning into a jungle: wild hyenas, wolves and a crocodile running loose; kids climbing to safety.

### Frame 4 · #334 DOMESTICATE (4:3)
A girl in a crimson bandana offering a date calmly to a wild hyena, slowly calming it; kids watching tensely.

### Frame 5 · #335 PREDATORY (4:3)
A shadow creature slinking up behind a boy like a hunting predator, eyes on his satchel.

### Frame 6 · #336 LETHARGIC (4:3)
A small ink djinn transformed into floating musical notes of a lullaby; a shadow creature yawning and slumping lazily.

### Frame 7 · #337 PLACATE (4:3)
An old sailor offering a leg of lamb and singing to a snarling lion, which lies down and purrs.

### Frame 8 · #338 MENAGERIE (4:3)
A zoo bursting back into color: giraffes, peacocks, lions and a trumpeting elephant; kids cheering.

### Frame 9 · #339 TIMID (4:3)
A shy baby gazelle peeking out from behind an elephant with a spark in its mouth; a boy kneeling patiently, hand outstretched.

### Frame 0 · #340 PRIMAL (wide 4:1)
All the zoo animals raising their heads to roar together into the sky; kids amazed; in the distance a café glowing with lanterns and music.
