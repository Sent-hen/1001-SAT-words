# Chapter 43 · Soldiers of Stone — Image prompts
📍 The Guard Barracks · Words 421–430

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #421 RIGID (4:3)
A barracks drill yard full of city guards turned to grey stone mid-march, rigid; kids walking between them.

### Frame 2 · #422 STOIC (4:3)
A half-stone captain gritting his teeth without complaint as stone creeps up his arm; kids rushing to him.

### Frame 3 · #423 IMPASSIVE (4:3)
A towering stone commander statue staring blankly with no emotion; kids staring back uneasily.

### Frame 4 · #424 DISCIPLINE (4:3)
A half-stone captain teaching kids a precise guard drill; kids standing in orderly rows, focused.

### Frame 5 · #425 REGIMENT (4:3)
Rows and rows of four hundred stone soldiers standing in formation; the half-stone captain pointing at them.

### Frame 6 · #426 HIERARCHY (4:3)
A girl scholar sketching a ranked pyramid chart of soldiers; the commander statue at its top.

### Frame 7 · #427 SUBORDINATE (4:3)
A half-stone captain looking up respectfully at a stone commander, shaking his head.

### Frame 8 · #428 COMPLIANT (4:3)
A boy whispering a word into a stone commander's ear as black ink flows into it; the commander's eyes glowing and waking obediently.

### Frame 9 · #429 INSUBORDINATE (4:3)
A shadow creature bursting from a stone commander's armor, shrieking and waving at the soldiers to rebel.

### Frame 0 · #430 MARTIAL (wide 4:1)
A stone commander awakened, drawing his sword; four hundred soldiers marching out to war drums; kids cheering; some wounded soldiers turning grey.
