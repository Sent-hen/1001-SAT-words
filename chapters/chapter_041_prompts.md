# Chapter 41 · Riddle of the Brass Lion — Image prompts
📍 The Basra Gate · Words 401–410

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #401 DEDUCE (4:3)
At dawn, a giant brass lion statue on a city gate rising with grinding gears and glowing turquoise eyes, addressing three kids and their friends below; riddles in glowing script.

### Frame 2 · #402 INFER (4:3)
A giant brass lion examining the kids closely, noticing ink on boots and smoke in hair; the kids looking at themselves in surprise.

### Frame 3 · #403 PREMISE (4:3)
A brass lion posing a logic puzzle with floating glowing statements; a boy with an orange headband thinking hard.

### Frame 4 · #404 FALLACY (4:3)
A boy confidently pointing up at the brass lion, explaining; the lion's gears whirring, then its face softening into a purr.

### Frame 5 · #405 RATIONAL (4:3)
A girl scholar solving a two-doors logic riddle in front of a brass lion; glowing doors, one with a lying guard.

### Frame 6 · #406 VALID (4:3)
The brass lion nodding approval; then turning its glowing eyes toward an old sailor with a braided white beard.

### Frame 7 · #407 HYPOTHESIS (4:3)
An old sailor offering a thoughtful guess to the brass lion; the lion considering him with a raised brass eyebrow.

### Frame 8 · #408 CONCLUSIVE (4:3)
An old sailor fearlessly patting a giant brass lion's paw; the lion purring; the great gate slowly groaning open.

### Frame 9 · #409 ACUMEN (4:3)
The brass lion gently giving a glowing brass whisker to a girl scholar; the whisker pointing like a compass.

### Frame 0 · #410 ASTUTE (wide 4:1)
A boy narrowing his eyes, noticing the brass lion's eyes flicker violet; behind the open gate, glowing monster eyes rising from a dark moat.
