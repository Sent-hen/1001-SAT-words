# Chapter 22 · Saffron and Sabotage — Image prompts
📍 The Street of Spices · Words 211–220

**Style prefix (put before every prompt):** Vibrant modern comic-book panel, Arabian Nights fantasy, mythic medieval Baghdad, bold clean ink outlines, cel shading, warm jewel-tone palette (saffron, teal, indigo, gold), cinematic angle, expressive faces, aimed at teenagers, no text.

**Character key: Zayn = 15-year-old boy, wild black curls, orange headband with trailing tails, saffron sleeveless tunic, teal trousers, courier satchel. Layla = 15-year-old girl scholar, loose indigo headscarf, reed pen behind ear, long black braid, turquoise ink-spotted robe, brass spectacles, glowing leather book on a strap. Qalam = small djinn of flowing black calligraphy ink with big golden eyes and a gold pen-nib crest. Tariq = 10-year-old boy, curls, missing front tooth, oversized lime-green cap. Sinbad = 70-year-old sailor, huge white beard braided into anchors, red-and-white striped turban, patched blue coat, whale-bone cane. Pebble = baby roc, fluffy sunset-red and gold, eggshell on head (grows to house-size). Marjana = 16-year-old girl, crimson bandana, bandolier of throwing knives, gold coin belt. Yasmin = 16-year-old girl, white heron-feather hooded cloak, silver winged eyeliner, one grey streak in black hair, crescent pendant. Zulmat = very tall vizier, violet-black robes fading to smoke, silver mask with no mouth, staff topped with black glowing meteorite. Hushlings = mouthless dripping ink-shadow creatures with pale hollow eyes.**

### Frame 1 · #211 SABOTAGE (4:3)
A spice street with towering colorful spice pyramids; merchants collapsing asleep from glittery purple powder; shadow creatures carrying them away.

### Frame 2 · #212 SINISTER (4:3)
A tall, smooth-faced man in a black silk turban with a silver tongue-shaped brooch, smiling sinisterly from shadows at the end of a spice street.

### Frame 3 · #213 SUBVERT (4:3)
A sinister auctioneer whispering rumors to merchants who glare at each other with suspicion; trust crumbling.

### Frame 4 · #214 MEDDLE (4:3)
A small boy in a green cap knocking over a tower of bright yellow turmeric while fiddling with scales; his brother groaning.

### Frame 5 · #215 UNDERMINE (4:3)
Shadow creatures tunneling under towering spice pyramids, which lean dangerously; kids pointing in alarm.

### Frame 6 · #216 TAMPER (4:3)
Orphan kids with grey feathers examining spice jars with broken and resealed purple wax seals; a raccoon-like paw print.

### Frame 7 · #217 MALICIOUS (4:3)
A girl scholar holding up a poisoned saffron jar, face serious; purple smoke; the sinister auctioneer's shadow on the wall.

### Frame 8 · #218 CULPRIT (4:3)
Orphan kids in grey feathers surrounding a guilty spice merchant with purple wax spilling from his pockets; a girl in a crimson bandana pointing a knife.

### Frame 9 · #219 CUNNING (4:3)
A sinister auctioneer smirking while secretly pouring glittering purple powder into a fountain in a distant square; kids realizing too late.

### Frame 0 · #220 THWART (wide 4:1)
A small ink djinn sealing a fountain with a dome of black ink while purple powder fizzles; kids cheering; in the background a boy in a green cap climbing onto a rolled carpet that twitches.
